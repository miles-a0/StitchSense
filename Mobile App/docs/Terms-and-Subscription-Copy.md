## Plain-English Terms Draft

### 1. Service

StitchSense Pro provides pattern storage, AI-assisted pattern support, rewrite tools, image analysis, and related workflow features.

### 2. Accounts

Users are responsible for keeping account login details secure and for activity on their account.

### 3. User Content

Users keep ownership of uploaded pattern files, images, prompts, and notes. Users grant StitchSense Pro permission to store, process, and transmit that content as needed to provide the service, including AI workflow processing.

### 4. AI Output

AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for a specific project. Users should check stitch counts, sizing, safety, yarn quantities, and construction details before relying on rewritten or analysed instructions.

### 5. Subscriptions

Paid Pro access renews automatically unless cancelled through the relevant payment provider. Web purchases are handled through Stripe. Mobile purchases are handled through Apple App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat used to sync entitlement status.

### 6. Free Trials and Complimentary Access

The standard trial gives full access for the stated trial period. Admins may grant complimentary or lifetime access to specific users. Complimentary access can be changed, revoked, or allowed to expire unless explicitly granted as lifetime access.

### 7. Cancellations

Users can cancel through the provider used for purchase. Cancelling prevents future renewals but does not necessarily refund the current billing period.

### 8. Acceptable Use

Users must not upload illegal content, attempt to bypass access controls, overload the service, reverse engineer private APIs, or use the service to violate intellectual property rights.

### 9. Availability

The service may be interrupted for maintenance, provider issues, or operational incidents.
